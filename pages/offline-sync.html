Offline copies on this computer

When this computer is offline, someone who has signed in here online can open a read-only copy of what their role could see, with the same password. Each copy is encrypted and opens only with its owner's password. A copy older than 7 days will not open.

Changes made offline

Not available in this version. The offline copy is read-only: a record cannot be created or changed while this computer is offline, and the screens say so. Nothing is waiting to be sent, so nothing can be lost here.

Saving changes offline and sending them to the shared database later (a queue per computer, safe numbering, and rules for money and stock) is the next phase. See OFFLINE_SYSTEM.md.

Before clearing browser data

Clearing this browser's site data (or "cookies and site data" for this site) removes the offline copies and the offline app. It removes nothing from the shared database. Everyone just needs to sign in online once again to make a new copy.
