  2. Roles & Permissions

Who may do what in the workshop system. Each user account has one role; the server checks the role's permissions on every request.

How access works

  • Super Admin is the protected system role: every permission, now and in future. It cannot be changed, switched off or deleted.
  • Every other role has exactly the permissions ticked here. A page or action the role does not have is hidden, and the server refuses it even if it is tried directly (by its address, or through the API).
  • Nobody can give a role, or an account, more than their own role has, and nobody can change the role they hold themselves.
  • The shared workshop passphrase still has full access, as before. Prefer personal accounts.
